My Research

Academic contributions to sustainability and environmental science.

Working Paper

A Hybrid ARDL-RSM Framework for Understanding the Impact of Energy, Urbanization, and Water Resources on the Ecological Footprint

This paper investigates the long-run and short-run dynamics between the Ecological Footprint and its key determinants — namely Alternative and Nuclear Energy Use, Renewable Internal Freshwater Resources, and Urban Population — within an environmental sustainability framework.

Using an ARDL model, this study examines the impact of these variables on the Ecological Footprint through energy consumption, resource management, and urbanization. The study further identifies limitations of the proposed model including data availability and statistical estimation approaches, and suggests machine learning applications for future research.

This work emphasizes the need to reconsider sustainable energy policies, resource management efficiency, and urban planning in achieving long-term environmental sustainability.
